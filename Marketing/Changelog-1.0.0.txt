Welcome to the first release of MarkdownArea!

• Write Markdown and see it formatted as you type. Only the line you are editing shows its raw Markdown.
• Headings, bold, italic, strikethrough, code, links, images, tables, lists, task lists with checkboxes, quotes, dividers and code blocks.
• A formatting toolbar appears when you move the mouse near the top edge.
• Switch between the formatted view and plain text at any time.
• Read-only mode with a gray background, for showing documents that should not be edited.
• Print your formatted documents.
• Full undo and redo, cut, copy and paste, and keyboard shortcuts for bold, italic and links.
• Works in light and dark mode and looks sharp on Retina and high-resolution screens.
• Smooth scrolling that stays fast even with long documents.
